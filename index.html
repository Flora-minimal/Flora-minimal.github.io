Why the sky burns gold

When the sun sits low, light travels a longer path through air. Short blue waves scatter away. Longer reds and ambers remain. That filter is why evenings feel warmer than noon.

LightSunset color is distance plus dust — Rayleigh scattering, then a little haze.
